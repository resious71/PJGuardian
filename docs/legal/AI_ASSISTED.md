# AI-assisted development

PJGuardian was developed with the help of an AI coding assistant (Claude Code). A person defined the requirements, split the work into staged prompts, reviewed every step's report, ran and checked the results, and decided what to commit.

What was checked by people and by automated tests is recorded in the repository:

- the automated test suites (PHP unit/integration tests, JavaScript tests, end-to-end and acceptance scenarios) and the browser checks described in `CONTRIBUTING.md`;
- `docs/spec_conformance.md` (requirements versus implementation) and `docs/unverified.md` (what has **not** been verified, such as Linux and real external identity providers).
